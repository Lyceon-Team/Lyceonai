## FINDINGS

### 1. LE2 sub-skill coverage gap (LE2.1, LE2.2, LE2.3 = 0)
All 300 LE2 questions are pure-math (abstract lines, slopes, intercepts,
perpendicular/parallel). NONE have contextual word problems, so:
  - LE2.1 (model from context): 0 — no questions ask to BUILD an equation from a word scenario
  - LE2.2 (interpret in context): 0 — no questions ask what a coefficient MEANS in a situation
  - LE2.3 (find quantity in context): 0 — no questions give one contextual variable to find another
This is a BANK GAP, not a tagging error. The LE2 bank contains only abstract
line-equation questions (LE2.4: connect representations, LE2.5: parallel/perp).
Contextual linear-equation questions may exist under other skill_codes or may be missing entirely.

### 2. LF.5 very low (8 tags)
LF.5 (intercepts and graph features) has only 8 tags across 294 LF questions.
Most intercept questions land in LF.3 (determine function from data) instead,
because they require computing the intercept from given information rather than
just reading/using it as a graph feature.
