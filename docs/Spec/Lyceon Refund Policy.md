## **Refund Policy at a Glance**

This summary provides a plain-English overview of how refunds work on Lyceon. It is not a substitute for the full policy below, which governs in case of any inconsistency.

**On your first paid subscription:** You have a 7-day satisfaction guarantee. If Lyceon isn't right for you, request a refund within 7 days of your initial paid subscription charge and we'll refund you in full.

**On automatic renewal charges:** If you forgot to cancel before a renewal charge and you haven't used the Service since the renewal, you have a 3-day grace window to request a refund. Beyond that, renewal refunds are reviewed case-by-case by our support team.

**If you live in the European Union, European Economic Area, or United Kingdom:** You have a statutory 14-day withdrawal right on distance sales that takes precedence over the 7-day window. You get whichever protection is more favorable.

**If you live in Quebec:** Quebec consumer-protection law gives you additional rights described in Section 6.2.

**How to request a refund:** Email **hello@lyceon.ai** with your account email and the charge you want refunded. We respond as quickly as possible — typically within a few business days.

**After a refund:** Your subscription is canceled and your access to paid features ends. Free-tier access remains.

**What we don't refund automatically:** Renewal charges outside the 3-day unused-service grace window; partial billing periods where you used the Service; chargebacks resolved against Lyceon. These are not categorical denials — contact us and we'll review the specifics of your situation.

## **2\. Definitions**

For purposes of this Policy:

**"Initial Subscription Charge"** means the first paid subscription charge on your Lyceon account. If you cancel and resubscribe at a later date, the first charge of your new subscription is treated as an Initial Subscription Charge.

**"Renewal Charge"** means any subscription charge after your Initial Subscription Charge, including monthly renewals, annual renewals, and any other recurring charges that occur automatically at the end of a billing period.

**"Satisfaction Window"** means the seven (7) calendar days immediately following an Initial Subscription Charge.

**"Renewal Grace Window"** means the three (3) calendar days immediately following a Renewal Charge during which a refund may be requested if you have not used the Service since the Renewal Charge occurred.

**"Used the Service"** means signing in to your Lyceon account, completing or beginning a practice question, interacting with our AI tutor LISA, opening any paid feature, or otherwise actively using the Service in a way that is recorded in our usage logs. Receiving a billing receipt or notification email does not count as Using the Service.

**"Billing Period"** means the period of time for which a subscription charge provides access (for example, one month for a monthly subscription, twelve months for an annual subscription).

**"EU/EEA/UK Distance Sale"** means a paid Lyceon subscription where you are a consumer (not a business) and you contracted with Lyceon without face-to-face interaction, as those terms are used in the EU Consumer Rights Directive and the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations.

## **3\. Satisfaction Guarantee — Initial Subscription Charges**

### **3.1 The Satisfaction Window**

If Lyceon isn't right for you, you may request a full refund of your Initial Subscription Charge within the Satisfaction Window (seven (7) calendar days following the charge).

To qualify:

* You must request the refund within seven (7) calendar days of the Initial Subscription Charge  
* You must contact us using the process described in Section 7  
* The refund applies to the Initial Subscription Charge only — Renewal Charges are governed by Section 4

Within the Satisfaction Window, we provide a **full refund** of the Initial Subscription Charge. We do not pro-rate based on the days you used the Service.

### **3.2 What Happens to Your Subscription**

When we process a refund under this Section, your subscription is canceled immediately and your access to paid features ends as soon as the cancellation is recorded in our systems. You retain access to free-tier features on the same account.

### **3.3 If You've Used the Service During the Satisfaction Window**

The satisfaction guarantee applies whether or not you've Used the Service during the Satisfaction Window. We don't ask you to prove you didn't try Lyceon — we expect you to try it. If you decide Lyceon isn't right for you within seven days, the refund is yours to request.

### **3.4 Resubscribing After a Satisfaction-Window Refund**

If you receive a Satisfaction Window refund and later resubscribe, the new subscription is treated as an Initial Subscription Charge with its own Satisfaction Window. Lyceon reserves the right, in our reasonable discretion, to decline repeated Satisfaction Window refunds where the pattern of use indicates the policy is being used in a way that materially diverges from its intended purpose. We apply this discretion consistently, based on objective account and billing history, and subject to applicable law.

## **4\. Renewal Charges — Grace Window for Unused Service**

### **4.1 The Renewal Grace Window**

If you forgot to cancel your subscription before a Renewal Charge and you have not Used the Service since the Renewal Charge occurred, you may request a full refund within the Renewal Grace Window (three (3) calendar days following the Renewal Charge).

To qualify:

* You must request the refund within three (3) calendar days of the Renewal Charge  
* You must not have Used the Service since the Renewal Charge — see Section 2 definition of "Used the Service"  
* You must contact us using the process described in Section 7

Within the Renewal Grace Window and where you have not Used the Service, we provide a **full refund** of the Renewal Charge.

### **4.2 If You've Used the Service After a Renewal**

If you have Used the Service in any way after a Renewal Charge, the Renewal Grace Window no longer applies. Renewal Charges where you have used the Service are reviewed under Section 5 (Renewal Charges Outside the Grace Window).

### **4.3 What Happens to Your Subscription**

When we process a refund under this Section, your subscription is canceled immediately and your access to paid features ends as soon as the cancellation is recorded in our systems. You retain access to free-tier features on the same account.

### **4.4 Cancelling Before a Renewal**

You can cancel a subscription at any time before a Renewal Charge through the Stripe customer portal accessible from your account settings, or by emailing **hello@lyceon.ai**. When you cancel before a Renewal Charge:

* The Renewal Charge does not occur  
* You retain access to paid features through the end of your current Billing Period  
* No refund is needed because no Renewal Charge was made

The Renewal Grace Window only applies when a Renewal Charge has already occurred and you discover the renewal after the fact.

## **5\. Renewal Charges Outside the Grace Window**

If your refund request does not qualify under Section 4 (because more than three (3) calendar days have passed since the Renewal Charge, or because you have Used the Service since the Renewal Charge), we will review your request on a case-by-case basis.

We consider, among other factors:

* How much time has passed since the Renewal Charge  
* Whether and how much you have used the Service since the Renewal Charge  
* Whether technical issues, unauthorized charges, billing errors, or other circumstances support a refund  
* Whether applicable consumer-protection law in your jurisdiction provides additional rights (see Section 6\)

We do not guarantee a refund in these cases, but we treat each request fairly and respond promptly. If we approve a refund under this Section, we may provide a full refund, a pro-rated refund based on the time remaining in the Billing Period, or a service credit toward future subscriptions, as appropriate to the circumstances.

If we decline a refund request under this Section, we provide a brief explanation of the basis for our decision and any options that may be available to you, including continuing to use the Service through the end of the Billing Period you have already paid for.

## **6\. Region-Specific Rights**

Consumer-protection law in certain jurisdictions gives you rights in addition to those described above. Where applicable law provides a refund right more favorable to you than this Policy, applicable law governs. The following Sections describe specific regional rights — they are summaries and are not legal advice; your actual rights are defined by the law of your jurisdiction.

### **6.1 European Union, European Economic Area, and United Kingdom — Distance-Selling Withdrawal Right**

If you reside in the European Union, European Economic Area, or United Kingdom and you contracted with Lyceon as a consumer through a distance-selling arrangement, you have a statutory right to withdraw from the contract within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of the date the contract was concluded, without giving any reason.

This right is provided by the EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU, as amended) and the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013\. It takes precedence over the seven (7)-day Satisfaction Window in Section 3 — you receive the more favorable of the two rights.

To exercise your withdrawal right:

* Send a clear statement of your decision to withdraw to **hello@lyceon.ai** within fourteen (14) calendar days of the date you subscribed  
* You may use the model withdrawal form provided in Annex I(B) of the EU Consumer Rights Directive, but you are not required to  
* We will refund all payments received from you for the subscription without undue delay and no later than fourteen (14) calendar days after the day on which we are informed of your decision to withdraw

**If you have started Using the Service during the withdrawal period:** By beginning to use a digital subscription service during the fourteen (14)-day withdrawal period, you may be deemed to have requested supply of the Service during the withdrawal period. Where applicable law treats this as a partial loss of withdrawal right (for example, under Article 16(m) of the EU Consumer Rights Directive concerning digital content), we will follow the requirements of applicable law and inform you of any consequences when you request to exercise your withdrawal right.

**Checkout disclosures and consent for digital content.** Before you complete a paid subscription as an EU/EEA/UK consumer, the Lyceon checkout flow presents the disclosures required by applicable consumer-protection law concerning your withdrawal right, including any required express consent and acknowledgment if your access to digital content begins during the statutory withdrawal period. Where applicable law requires specific consent or acknowledgment text, that text is presented at checkout in the form required by applicable law. The full disclosure presented at checkout is also reproduced in our **Subscription / Auto-Renewal Notice**.

### **6.2 Quebec — Consumer Protection Act**

If you reside in Quebec, the Quebec Consumer Protection Act ("**LPC**") and related regulations may give you additional rights with respect to your Lyceon subscription, including provisions concerning distance contracts under sections 54.1 to 54.16 of the LPC.

Where the LPC provides rights more favorable to you than this Policy, the LPC governs. To exercise any LPC-based right, contact us using the process described in Section 7\.

### **6.3 California — Automatic Renewal Law**

If you reside in California, the California Automatic Renewal Law (California Business and Professions Code § 17600 et seq.) provides specific protections for automatically renewing subscriptions, including:

* Clear and conspicuous disclosure of recurring-charge terms before you subscribe  
* Affirmative consent to auto-renewal terms separate from broader Terms of Service acceptance  
* An easy and prominent online cancellation mechanism for online subscriptions  
* Reminder notices for long-term auto-renewals

These protections are addressed in our **Subscription / Auto-Renewal Notice**. Where the California Automatic Renewal Law provides rights more favorable to you than this Policy, that law governs.

### **6.4 Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore**

Australian consumers retain rights under the Australian Consumer Law (Schedule 2 to the Competition and Consumer Act 2010), New Zealand consumers retain rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, and Singapore consumers retain rights under the Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act 2003\. These rights cannot be waived by contract. Where applicable consumer-protection law provides rights more favorable to you than this Policy, that law governs.

### **6.5 Other Jurisdictions**

If you reside in a jurisdiction not specifically addressed above, applicable consumer-protection law in your jurisdiction may give you rights in addition to those described in this Policy. Where applicable law provides a refund right more favorable to you than this Policy, applicable law governs.

## **7\. How to Request a Refund**

### **7.1 Contact**

To request a refund, email **hello@lyceon.ai** from the email address associated with your Lyceon account. Please include:

* The email address on your Lyceon account  
* The date and amount of the charge you want refunded  
* A brief explanation of why you are requesting a refund  
* For refunds requested under Section 4 (Renewal Grace Window), a confirmation that you have not Used the Service since the Renewal Charge

We do not require a detailed reason for a refund request within the Satisfaction Window or the Renewal Grace Window. We ask for context to improve the Service and to help our support team respond effectively.

### **7.2 Self-Serve Cancellation vs. Refund Request**

You can cancel your subscription yourself at any time through the Stripe customer portal accessible from your Lyceon account settings. **Self-serve cancellation stops future Renewal Charges but does not, by itself, refund a charge you've already paid.** To request a refund of a charge that has already occurred, email **hello@lyceon.ai** as described in Section 7.1.

If you cancel through the customer portal during the Satisfaction Window or Renewal Grace Window and you also want a refund of a charge that has already occurred, you must still email us to request the refund. We do not automatically refund charges based on cancellation alone, because not every user who cancels wants a refund — some prefer to keep using the Service through the end of the Billing Period they have already paid for.

### **7.3 Response Time**

We respond to refund requests as quickly as possible. For requests within the Satisfaction Window or the Renewal Grace Window, we aim to process the refund within a few business days. For requests under Section 5 (case-by-case review) or Section 6 (region-specific rights), we may take longer to investigate the circumstances, but we will keep you informed of the status of your request.

### **7.4 Refund Method**

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the charge, where technically available. If the original payment method is no longer available (for example, the card has expired or been closed), we will handle the refund using a legally permitted alternative method, which may include another payment method on file, another payment method you provide, or, only where permitted by applicable law and accepted by you, a service credit toward future Lyceon subscriptions. We do not issue refunds in cash, by check, or to a different person than the original payer.

## **9\. Chargebacks**

A "**chargeback**" is when you dispute a charge directly with your bank, card issuer, or payment network rather than requesting a refund from us.

We prefer to resolve refund and billing concerns directly. Direct resolution is typically faster than the chargeback process, doesn't affect your account standing, and lets us investigate the underlying issue (for example, a billing error or unauthorized charge) more thoroughly than a chargeback usually allows.

If you initiate a chargeback for a Lyceon charge:

* We may pause access to the Service on the disputed account pending investigation  
* We may provide documentation to your payment provider to address the dispute  
* Chargebacks resolved against Lyceon do not require us to issue an additional refund of the same charge  
* Repeated chargebacks across multiple charges may result in account review and, where appropriate, account closure under our Student Terms of Use or Parent / Guardian Terms

If you have already initiated a chargeback and want to resolve the matter directly with us instead, contact **hello@lyceon.ai** and we will work with you to withdraw the chargeback and process a direct refund where appropriate.

## **10\. Non-Refundable Items and Edge Cases**

Certain items are not subject to refund under this Policy, except where applicable consumer-protection law requires otherwise:

* **Third-party fees:** If your payment provider (bank, card issuer, payment network) charged you fees for currency conversion, foreign transaction processing, or similar services in connection with your Lyceon charge, those third-party fees are not refundable by Lyceon. You may be able to recover them from your payment provider directly.  
* **Promotional credits, gift subscriptions, and scholarships:** If your subscription was paid for with a promotional credit, a gift subscription, or a scholarship provided by Lyceon or a third party, refunds are limited to the actual amount you paid out of pocket, if any. We do not refund the cash value of promotional credits, gift subscriptions, or scholarships back to you.  
* **Account closures for material violations of our terms:** If your account is closed for a material violation of our Student Terms of Use, Parent / Guardian Terms, Honor Code, or Acceptable Use Policy, we generally do not provide a refund of subscription fees for the remainder of the Billing Period, unless required by applicable law or determined otherwise by Lyceon in our reasonable discretion. This is a contractual consequence of the violation, not a refund-related decision.  
* **Currency fluctuations:** Refunds are issued in the currency of the original charge. If exchange rates have changed between the date of the charge and the date of the refund, the refund amount in a different currency may differ from the amount of the original charge in that currency.

This list is not exhaustive. We treat refund requests outside the Satisfaction Window and Renewal Grace Window on a case-by-case basis as described in Section 5, including for situations that are not specifically listed above.

## **11\. Refund Processing Time**

Once we approve a refund, we initiate the refund through Stripe, our payment processor. The time it takes for the refund to appear in your account depends on the payment method and the policies of your bank or card issuer:

* **Credit and debit cards:** Typically five (5) to ten (10) business days after we initiate the refund  
* **Bank transfers and direct debit (where applicable):** Typically five (5) to fifteen (15) business days  
* **Other payment methods:** Timing varies depending on the payment method and the policies of the issuing institution

We do not control how long your bank or card issuer takes to credit a refund to your account once we have initiated it. If a refund has not appeared in your account within fifteen (15) business days of our confirming the refund has been issued, contact us at **hello@lyceon.ai** and we will investigate.

For refunds under EU/UK statutory withdrawal rights (Section 6.1), we initiate the refund without undue delay and no later than fourteen (14) calendar days after we are informed of your decision to withdraw, as required by applicable law.
