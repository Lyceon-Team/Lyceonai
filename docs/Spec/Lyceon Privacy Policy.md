## **Privacy at a Glance**

This summary provides a plain-English overview of how Lyceon handles your information. It is not a substitute for the full Privacy Policy below, which governs in case of any inconsistency.

**What Lyceon is.** Lyceon is an artificial-intelligence-powered SAT preparation platform serving students aged 13 and older, with optional Parent/Guardian accounts that provide supervisory visibility. Lyceon's AI tutor is named LISA.

**What we do with your data.** We use your information to provide and improve the Service, to personalize learning to your demonstrated knowledge and target exam date, to support LISA conversations, to operate billing for paid subscriptions, to keep the Service secure, and to comply with legal obligations. After account deletion or retention-window expiration, we may retain anonymized, non-personal records for product improvement, learning-effectiveness research, safety, and AI-quality improvement, as described below.

**What we don't do.**

* We do not sell your personal information.  
* We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.  
* We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising.  
* We do not display third-party advertising on the Service.  
* We do not allow our sub-processors (the vendors that help operate Lyceon) to use Lyceon data to train their own general-purpose AI models.  
* We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or permit under-13 accounts. If we discover that a user is under 13, we delete the account and associated data across our systems, subject only to legally required financial, security, fraud-prevention, tax, audit, refund, chargeback, or payment-network records where applicable.

**About AI on Lyceon.** LISA explanations, hints, and feedback are generated by an artificial intelligence system. AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or otherwise inappropriate, including "hallucinated" content (content the system presents confidently but that is false). LISA is not professional, academic, medical, or legal advice. We retain AI-related conversation data for specific purposes and durations described in Section 9 below.

**Children, minors, and guardians.**

* Under 13: blocked at signup; deleted if detected after the fact.  
* Ages 13–17: allowed with required Parent/Guardian acknowledgment; in jurisdictions where the digital age of consent is higher than 13 (notably Ireland at 16), parental consent applies for under-threshold users.  
* Under 18 in the United Kingdom and Australia: protected by age-appropriate design obligations (the UK Children's Code and the Australian Children's Online Privacy Code respectively).  
* Parents and guardians may exercise privacy rights on behalf of their minor Student Users.

**How long we keep your information.** Information attributable to your specific account is retained for 12 months from your last activity and then deleted through our cascade deletion process. After account deletion or retention-window expiration, we may retain anonymized, non-personal structured learning records for product improvement, learning-effectiveness research, safety, and AI-quality improvement, as described below. Financial records processed through Stripe are retained for periods appropriate to financial recordkeeping and audit obligations.

**Your rights.** Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict our processing of your personal information; to object to certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to be free from unlawful discrimination for exercising privacy rights. Section 11 explains these rights and how to exercise them.

**How to contact us.** Email **hello@lyceon.ai** for privacy inquiries, rights requests, or questions about this Policy. Section 16 contains additional contact information including regional supervisory-authority complaint paths.

## **1\. Definitions**

For purposes of this Policy:

**"Aggregated Data"** means information that has been combined with data from other individuals such that it does not identify any particular individual.

**"AI Content"** means content generated by Lyceon's artificial intelligence systems, including explanations, hints, walkthroughs, feedback, and instructional material produced by LISA.

**"Age-Appropriate Design User"** means a user who is covered by child-safety or age-appropriate-design frameworks, including the UK Children's Code and the Australian Children's Online Privacy Code (each of which generally applies to users under eighteen (18) in those jurisdictions).

**"COPPA Child"** or **"Child"** means an individual under thirteen (13) years of age for purposes of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and Lyceon's under-13 deletion posture described in Section 9.4.

**"De-identified Data"** means information that cannot reasonably be used to identify, relate to, describe, or be linked to a particular individual, where Lyceon (i) takes reasonable measures to ensure the data cannot be associated with an individual, (ii) publicly commits to maintain and use the data only in de-identified form, and (iii) contractually obligates any recipients to comply with the foregoing.

**"Digital Consent Threshold"** means the age at which a user may consent to certain processing of Personal Information under applicable law. The threshold varies by jurisdiction, for example sixteen (16) in Ireland and certain other EU member states; thirteen (13) in the United Kingdom and some other jurisdictions. Where the Digital Consent Threshold exceeds the user's age and Lyceon relies on consent as the legal basis for processing, parental or guardian consent applies.

**"LISA"** means Lyceon's AI-powered learning assistant, which provides explanations, hints, walkthroughs, and feedback to support Student learning.

**"Minor"** means an individual under eighteen (18) years of age. All Minor Student Users operate under our **Parent / Guardian Terms**.

**"Personal Information"** or **"Personal Data"** means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular individual or household. The specific scope of Personal Information may vary by jurisdiction. Personal Information does not include De-identified Data or Aggregated Data.

**"Anonymized Structured Learning Data"** means structured learning-event records derived from practice, review, assessment, and mastery activity that have been processed so they are no longer associated with a user account and cannot reasonably be used by Lyceon to identify, relate to, describe, or be linked to a particular individual or household. Anonymized Structured Learning Data is not Personal Information and does not include LISA free-text conversation content unless Lyceon has implemented and verified a separate anonymization process for that content.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Information, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, dissemination, restriction, erasure, or destruction.

**"Sensitive Personal Information"** means categories of Personal Information that receive heightened protection under applicable law, which may include government-issued identifiers, precise geolocation, racial or ethnic origin, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual orientation, health or medical information, genetic data, biometric data used for identification, and financial-account credentials. The specific scope varies by jurisdiction.

**"Sub-Processor"** means a third party engaged by Lyceon to process Personal Information on Lyceon's behalf in connection with providing the Service.

Lyceon operates the Service from the United States. By using the Service, you acknowledge that such international transfers occur subject to the safeguards described in Section 12 (International Data Transfers).

### **2.2 Age Eligibility**

The Service is intended for use by individuals who are at least thirteen (13) years of age. Lyceon does not knowingly create paid subscription accounts for individuals under thirteen (13). If we become aware that an individual under thirteen (13) has created an account, the account is suspended or terminated and associated Personal Information is deleted in accordance with applicable law (see Section 9.4).

Lyceon's legal-document program uses four distinct age-related concepts (see Section 1 Definitions) that should not be conflated:

* **COPPA Child** (under 13): Subject to Lyceon's no-paid-account posture and to deletion under Section 9.4 if detected. Data is excluded from our AI training corpus.  
* **Digital Consent Threshold** (varies by jurisdiction): Where applicable law sets a Digital Consent Threshold higher than thirteen (13), and where consent is the legal basis for our Processing of a user's Personal Information, parental or guardian consent is required for users below the local threshold. The threshold varies by EU member state (sixteen (16) in Ireland, Germany, the Netherlands, and others; thirteen (13) in some others; intermediate ages in some). Where valid parental or guardian consent is obtained, the account may continue to operate; absence of required consent results in account suspension or deletion as appropriate.  
* **Age-Appropriate Design User** (typically under 18): In jurisdictions with age-appropriate-design frameworks (notably the UK Children's Code; the Australian Children's Online Privacy Code being established by the OAIC; and similar emerging frameworks), Lyceon applies design and disclosure obligations appropriate to the age of the user. These obligations affect how we design and disclose; they are not deletion triggers.  
* **Minor Student User** (under 18): All Minor Student Users operate under the **Parent / Guardian Terms** with a guardian-visibility model and require parental or guardian acknowledgment through our parental consent flow at signup.

**Lyceon does not currently implement a "verifiable parental consent" mechanism within the meaning of COPPA Section 312.5.** Our current posture is to block paid accounts for users under thirteen (13) rather than to obtain COPPA-grade verifiable parental consent for such users. For users at or above thirteen (13), we use clickwrap parental or guardian acknowledgment as part of our parental consent flow at signup.

If you are a Parent or Guardian and believe your child has provided Personal Information to Lyceon in violation of these provisions, please contact us using the information in Section 16\.

### **2.3 Relationship to Other Documents**

This Policy operates together with our **Student Terms of Use**, **Parent / Guardian Terms**, **Children's Online Privacy Notice**, **Cookie Policy**, **Sub-Processor List**, **Refund Policy**, **Subscription / Auto-Renewal Notice**, **Acceptable Use Policy**, **Honor Code**, and **Community Guidelines**. LISA is Lyceon's AI-powered learning assistant. When you interact with LISA, the following applies:

**What is captured:** The text of your messages to LISA, LISA's responses, and event-level metadata (timestamps, conversation identifiers, and the practice context in which the conversation occurred).

**How it is used:** LISA conversation content is used to provide the conversational experience in real time, to identify patterns in tutor effectiveness for product improvement, and as described in Section 4.3 (Use for AI Improvement).

**How long it is retained:** LISA conversation content is retained according to LISA-specific retention windows distinct from general Personal Information retention. See Section 9.5 for the retention summary covering LISA-related data categories.

**Personally identifiable content in LISA conversations:** You should avoid sharing personal contact information, real-world identifiers, or other sensitive Personal Information in LISA conversations. **AI Content disclosure:** LISA-generated content is created by an artificial intelligence system. Additional automated-systems and AI-specific disclosures, including disclosures required under Article 50 of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (applicable from August 2, 2026\) and under Australian automated decision-making disclosure requirements (effective December 10, 2026), are provided in Section 8 of this Policy.

* Conduct internal research on learning effectiveness  
* Train, fine-tune, and evaluate AI models that power the Service

## **8\. Automated Systems, AI, and Personalization**

The mastery engine selects questions adaptively, customizes the difficulty of upcoming practice, prioritizes practice topics, and feeds the personalization described in Section 4.2. The mastery engine is a deterministic statistical system; it is not a generative AI system, but it is an automated system whose outputs affect your in-product experience.

**Scoring Formula.** Lyceon's scoring formula produces estimated SAT scores from your practice exam performance. The scoring formula is a deterministic statistical formula and is not a generative AI system. Estimated scores are predictions of likely real-exam performance based on Lyceon's measurement methodology; they are not actual SAT scores issued by the College Board.

## **9\. Retention and Deletion**

### **9.1 General Retention Approach**

We retain Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to provide the Service, to satisfy legal and regulatory obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements.

Specific retention periods by data category are summarized in Section 9.7 below.

### **9.2 Account Inactivity Retention**

Personal Information attributable to your specific account is retained for **twelve (12) months from your last activity**. After twelve (12) months of inactivity, your account-attributable Personal Information is automatically deleted through our cascade deletion process.

"Last activity" is determined based on the canonical activity timestamp in our authentication system. Logging into the Service, completing practice questions, interacting with LISA, or modifying account settings all constitute activity.

### **9.3 User-Initiated Account Deletion**

You may request deletion of your account at any time through your account settings or by contacting us. Upon your deletion request:

* Your account is placed in a soft-deleted state immediately, suspending access  
* After a seven (7)-day soft-delete window during which you may request restoration, your account and Personal Information are permanently deleted through our cascade deletion process  
* After the seven (7)-day soft-delete window, Lyceon permanently deletes your account and account-attributable Personal Information from active Lyceon systems through our cascade deletion process, subject to limited exceptions for legally required financial, security, fraud-prevention, tax, audit, payment-network, dispute, backup, and vendor records described in this Policy
* Structured learning-event records may be retained only after they have been anonymized so they are no longer associated with your account and are no longer Personal Information

If you have an active paid subscription at the time of deletion, your subscription is cancelled at the end of the current paid billing period. You retain access through the end of the current period unless you request immediate cancellation. We do not continue to bill subscriptions after account deletion.

### **9.4 Children's Data Deletion**

Where we determine that a user is under thirteen (13) years of age, the account undergoes hard deletion across all Lyceon systems. This includes:

* Account and learning activity data  
* Analytics event data attributable to the account  
* LISA conversation data  
* Exclusion from any retained anonymized records used for AI training

This "hard delete everywhere" treatment is more aggressive than the cascade deletion applied to adult accounts and reflects the heightened protection appropriate to children under thirteen (13).

**Limited exception for legally required records.** If an under-13 account has generated billing, refund, chargeback, fraud-prevention, tax, audit, payment-network, or analogous records subject to legal or accounting retention obligations, Lyceon retains only the minimum financial or security records required by law, accounting obligations, payment-network rules, or dispute defense. Non-required child personal information is deleted or minimized as early as vendor mechanics and legal obligations allow. See Section 9.6 for the general retention treatment of financial records.

**For users above thirteen (13) who are below an applicable jurisdictional Digital Consent Threshold (for example, users aged thirteen (13) to fifteen (15) in Ireland or in EU member states with a higher Digital Consent Threshold), the operational treatment is suspension or deletion only if required parental or guardian consent is not obtained.** Where valid parental or guardian consent is obtained through our parental consent flow at signup, the account may continue to operate; the account is not hard-deleted merely because the user falls below a local Digital Consent Threshold. This distinction reflects that GDPR Article 8 and similar frameworks contemplate parental consent as the operational path for under-threshold users, not automatic deletion.

For Student Users in jurisdictions with age-appropriate-design frameworks (notably the UK Children's Code and the Australian Children's Online Privacy Code), the framework's design and disclosure obligations apply. These obligations are not deletion triggers.
