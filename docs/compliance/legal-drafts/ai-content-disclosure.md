# **LYCEON AI Content Disclosure**

LYCEON uses artificial intelligence (AI) in parts of the service. This notice tells you where, what that means, and what to do if something looks wrong.

---

## **1. Where AI Is Used**

* **LISA, the AI tutor.** LISA's explanations, hints and replies are **generated by AI**. LISA is available to students aged 13 and over with a paid subscription. It is never available to anyone under 13.
* **Automated results.** Practice scores, test scores and progress summaries are **calculated automatically** by our software from the questions you answer.

Content you see in LYCEON that is not marked as coming from LISA — questions, answer explanations and articles — is prepared by LYCEON.

---

## **2. What AI Content Is, and Is Not**

* **AI can be wrong.** LISA may give an explanation that is incomplete, unclear or incorrect. Check its reasoning against the question and your own work.
* **It is a study aid.** LISA helps you understand; it is not a teacher, an official SAT source, or professional advice of any kind.
* **It does not decide anything about you.** Nothing LISA says changes your scores, your progress or your account.
* **Automated results are estimates for practice.** Practice and test scores in LYCEON are not official SAT scores.

---

## **3. Safety**

We use automated safety checks and human review to keep LISA conversations appropriate for students. If a conversation suggests someone may be at risk, we may review it and act as described in **Trust & Safety at LYCEON**.

---

## **4. Your Information**

How tutor conversations are handled, who processes them, and how long they are kept is set out in the **LYCEON Privacy Policy** (Sections 3, 5 and 6). Conversations are not used to train public or general-purpose AI models.

---

## **5. Tell Us If Something Is Wrong**

If LISA says something incorrect, inappropriate or worrying, use the report option in the conversation or email **support@lyceon.ai**. We review reports and use them to improve the service.

---

## **6. Changes**

AI rules are changing in several countries. We will update this notice as they do, and note the date.
