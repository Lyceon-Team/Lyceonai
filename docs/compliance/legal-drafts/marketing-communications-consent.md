# **LYCEON Marketing Communications Consent**

**Version 1** · **Effective date:** [●]

## **1. Consent Checkbox**

Shown unticked at sign-up and in account settings, separately from acceptance of the Terms. It is not required to use LYCEON.

> ☐ **Send me occasional emails from LYCEON** with study tips, SAT news and updates about new features. I can unsubscribe at any time.

Shown below the checkbox:

> We will still send emails about your account, such as sign-in, billing and safety messages. See our **Privacy Policy**.

The checkbox is not shown to users under 13.

## **2. What We Send**

If you opt in, we may send:

* study tips and SAT preparation guides;
* SAT news relevant to students or parents;
* updates about new LYCEON features; and
* offers relating to LYCEON subscriptions.

## **3. Service Messages**

Messages about your account and the service do not require this consent. These include sign-in and security messages, billing and renewal reminders, receipts, safety messages, changes to our terms, and notifications you have turned on.

## **4. Unsubscribing**

* **Unsubscribe link:** every marketing email contains an **Unsubscribe** link. One click stops marketing emails; you do not need to sign in.
* **Account settings:** turn **Marketing emails** on or off at any time.
* **Email:** **support@lyceon.ai**.

We process unsubscribe requests promptly, and in any case within 10 business days.

## **5. Who Does Not Receive Marketing**

* Users under 13.
* Anyone who has unsubscribed or asked us not to contact them.

## **6. Sender Identification**

Each marketing email identifies LYCEON as the sender, includes our postal address, [POSTAL ADDRESS], and contains an unsubscribe link.

## **7. Consent Records**

We record your choice, the date and time you made it, and where you made it.
