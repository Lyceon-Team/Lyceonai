# **LYCEON School Data Privacy Addendum**

**Version 1** · **Effective date:** 2026-10-07

## **1. Data Privacy Agreement**

Where a school, district or other educational agency (the "School") uses LYCEON for its students, LYCEON enters into the **Student Data Privacy Consortium (SDPC) National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA)**, version 2, with the state-specific exhibit for the School's state where one exists. That agreement governs. This addendum summarizes its commitments.

Where the School uses its own data privacy agreement, LYCEON will review it against the same commitments.

## **2. Our Role**

* **School official.** LYCEON acts as a "school official" with a "legitimate educational interest" under FERPA (34 CFR §99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)). We perform a service the School would otherwise perform itself, we are under the School's direct control with respect to the use and maintenance of education records, and we use them only for authorized purposes.
* **School control.** Student data provided by or through the School remains under the School's control. LYCEON processes it only to provide the service to the School.
* **Students under 13.** Where the School authorizes LYCEON's use for its students, LYCEON relies on that authorization in the school context only, consistent with FTC COPPA guidance, and uses the data only for the School's educational purpose.

## **3. Our Commitments**

* **No sale or advertising.** We do not sell student data, use it for targeted advertising, or build profiles for non-educational purposes.
* **Use limitation.** We use student data only to provide the service under the agreement.
* **Sub-processors.** We bind each sub-processor to obligations at least as protective as ours. They are listed in the **LYCEON Sub-Processor List**.
* **Security.** We maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, as named in the agreement's security exhibit.
* **Breach notification.** We notify the School of a data breach affecting its student data within the period required by the agreement or applicable state law, whichever is shorter.
* **Access and correction.** We assist the School in responding to requests from parents and eligible students to review or correct education records.
* **Return and deletion.** When the agreement ends, or on the School's written request, we return or delete the School's student data within the period set by the agreement, and certify deletion on request. De-identified data is retained only as the agreement permits.
* **No re-disclosure.** We do not disclose student data except as the agreement permits or the law requires.

## **4. School Access**

The School's authorized staff can view the progress and activity of the School's own students, as set out in the agreement.

## Decisions (Karl, 2026-10-07)

1. School authorization wording (Section 2): kept as written; it follows the FTC's school-authorization guidance (COPPA FAQ, Section N).
2. De-identified data after termination: retained only as the signed agreement permits (the NDPA default).
3. Security framework for the NDPA security exhibit: the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
4. State exhibits: none prepared in advance; the exhibit for the contracting school's state is used when a school signs.
5. Publication: not published; supplied to a school on request and signed per school.
