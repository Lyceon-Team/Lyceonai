# **LYCEON School Data Privacy Addendum**

## **1. The agreement we sign**

When a school, district or other educational agency ("the School") uses LYCEON for its students, LYCEON signs the **Student Data Privacy Consortium (SDPC) National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA)**, version 2, with the state-specific exhibit for the School's state, where one exists. That agreement governs. This addendum summarises the commitments in it and adds nothing beyond it.

If the School uses its own data privacy agreement, LYCEON will review it against the same commitments.

## **2. Our role**

* **School official.** LYCEON acts as a "school official" with a "legitimate educational interest" under FERPA (34 CFR §99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)): we perform a service the School would otherwise use its own staff for, we are under the School's direct control for the use and maintenance of education records, and we use them only for the authorised purpose.
* **The School controls the data.** Student data provided by or through the School remains the School's. LYCEON processes it only to provide the service to the School.
* **Consent for under-13 students.** Where the School authorises LYCEON's use for its students, LYCEON relies on that authorisation for the school context only, as the FTC's COPPA guidance describes, and uses the data only for the school's educational purpose.

## **3. Our commitments (as in the NDPA)**

* **No sale, no advertising.** We do not sell student data, use it for targeted advertising, or build a profile for any non-educational purpose.
* **Use limitation.** We use student data only to provide the service under the agreement.
* **Sub-processors.** We bind each sub-processor to obligations at least as protective as ours. Our current list is the **LYCEON Sub-Processor List**.
* **Security.** We maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, aligned with a recognised framework named in the agreement's security exhibit.
* **Breach notification.** We notify the School of a data breach affecting its student data within the period the agreement or state law requires, whichever is shorter.
* **Parent and student access.** We help the School respond to requests from parents and eligible students to review or correct education records.
* **Return and deletion.** When the agreement ends, or on the School's written request, we return or delete the School's student data within the period the agreement sets, and certify deletion on request. De-identified data is kept only as the agreement allows.
* **No re-disclosure.** We do not disclose student data except as the agreement allows or the law requires.

## **4. What the School sees**

The School's designated staff can see the progress and activity of their own students, as the agreement and its exhibits describe. **[Feature not built; describe at contract time.]**

## Counsel checklist

2. **De-identified data after termination.** Section 3 defers to the agreement. Decide LYCEON's standard position. Product behaviour today is that de-identified learning records survive account deletion (Privacy Policy v5 §6.2).
3. **Security framework.** Name the framework LYCEON will attest to in the NDPA security exhibit.
4. **State exhibits.** Decide which states' exhibits to prepare first, based on the sales pipeline.
5. **Publication.** Confirm this summary may be published on the website, or should be supplied to schools only on request.
