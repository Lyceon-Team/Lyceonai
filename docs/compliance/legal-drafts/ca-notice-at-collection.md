# **LYCEON California Notice at Collection**

If you live in California, this notice tells you, at or before the point we collect it, what personal information we collect, why, and for how long. Full details are in the **LYCEON Privacy Policy**.

---

## **What we collect, why, and how long we keep it**

| Category (California law) | Examples | Why we collect it | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Identifiers** | Name, email address, account ID, IP address | To create and run your account, keep it secure, and contact you about the service | While your account is open; see Privacy Policy §6.2 for what happens on deletion |
| **Customer records** | Billing name and address (held by Stripe), subscription records | To process payments and manage subscriptions | Payment records: 7 years (Privacy Policy §6.2) |
| **Characteristics of protected classifications** | Date of birth (age) | To apply age rules, including parental connection for under-13 students | While your account is open |
| **Commercial information** | Subscription purchased, billing period | To provide what you paid for | Payment records: 7 years |
| **Internet or other electronic network activity** | Pages used, device and browser data, log and error data; product analytics **[effective when F10/F11 ship, and only if you accept analytics cookies]** | To operate, secure and improve the service | Security records 365 days; other operational records up to 90 days (Privacy Policy §6.5, §6.7); analytics per Privacy Policy §6.6 |
| **Education information** | Practice answers, progress, test results | To provide the learning service and show progress | While your account is open; de-identified on deletion |
| **Audio, electronic, visual or similar** | Tutor conversations (text) with LISA, for students 13 and over | To provide the AI tutor | While your subscription is active, then 7 days (Privacy Policy §6.1) |
| **Sensitive personal information** | Account login (email or Google sign-in, with password or credential) | Only to let you sign in and keep the account secure | While your account is open |

We do not collect precise geolocation, government identifiers, financial account numbers (Stripe holds card details), health information, or biometric information.

## **Sale and sharing**

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.** This includes the information of consumers under 16. See the **LYCEON Notice of Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing**.

## **Sensitive personal information**

We use sensitive personal information only to provide the service you asked for and to keep it secure, so there is no use for you to limit.

## **Your rights**

You can ask to know, delete or correct your information, and you will not be treated differently for doing so. See Privacy Policy §8.1, or email **support@lyceon.ai**.

## Counsel checklist

1. **Categories.** Confirm the category mapping. In particular, whether learning activity is "education information" under §1798.140(v)(1)(J), which is defined by reference to FERPA, or belongs elsewhere for a direct-to-consumer service, and whether tutor conversation text belongs under "audio, electronic, visual or similar" or "inferences".
2. **Retention by category.** §7012(e)(4) requires a retention period, or the criteria for one, per category. Confirm that the cross-references to Privacy Policy §6 are specific enough.
3. **Age as a protected classification.** Date of birth is used only for age rules. Confirm the classification.
4. **Placement.** Confirm a link at sign-up and checkout is a conspicuous enough placement under §7012(c).
5. **Threshold.** Confirm whether LYCEON meets a CCPA applicability threshold at launch. Publishing voluntarily is industry practice either way.
