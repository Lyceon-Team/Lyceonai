# **LYCEON Children's Online Privacy Notice**

**Version 1** · **Effective date:** [●]

**For parents and guardians of students under 13**

This notice explains what LYCEON collects from a student under 13, how we use it, and the choices available to you. It forms part of the **LYCEON Privacy Policy**.

---

## **1. Who We Are**

LYCEON AI operates the LYCEON SAT preparation service.

* Email: **support@lyceon.ai**
* Postal address: [POSTAL ADDRESS]
* Telephone: [TELEPHONE]

---

## **2. Parent or Guardian Connection**

LYCEON is designed for students aged 13 and over. A student under 13 can use LYCEON's learning features only while a parent or guardian is connected to their account.

* **Before you connect**, your child's account can be used only to connect a parent or guardian and to manage the account. We hold only the information needed to create it: name, email address, date of birth and sign-in details, the record of the sign-up agreements, and technical data such as IP address.
* **If your child asks us to**, we email you their link code at the address they provide. We use your address only to send that email and to prevent misuse of this feature.
* **You connect** by entering your child's link code in your own LYCEON account and agreeing to the **LYCEON Parent / Guardian Terms**. You must be at least 18.
* **If you disconnect** and no other parent or guardian is connected, your child's learning features are no longer available.

---

## **3. Information We Collect**

* **Account information:** name, email address, date of birth and sign-in details.
* **Learning activity:** answers to practice and test questions, progress and time spent.
* **Technical information:** device and browser type, IP address, and the cookies needed to keep your child signed in.

We collect only the information reasonably necessary for your child to use LYCEON, and we do not require more information as a condition of participation.

---

## **4. What We Do Not Do**

* **No AI tutor.** LISA, our AI tutor, is not available to users under 13.
* **No analytics or session recording.** Accounts of users under 13 are excluded.
* **No marketing or review invitations.** We do not send them to users under 13.
* **No advertising.** We do not show advertising, use your child's information for advertising, or sell or share it.
* **No public profile.** Your child cannot make personal information publicly available.

---

## **5. How We Use Information**

We use your child's information to provide the service, show their practice and progress, show you the information you are entitled to see, keep the account secure, and maintain the service.

---

## **6. Disclosure**

* **To you, and any other connected parent or guardian**, as described in the Privacy Policy, Section 5.1.
* **To our service providers**, only on our instructions and only to provide the service. These are listed in the **LYCEON Sub-Processor List**.
* **Where required by law**, or to protect someone's safety.

---

## **7. Data Retention**

We keep your child's information while the account is open. When the account is deleted, we delete the information that identifies your child after 7 days and de-identify the remaining learning records. Our full retention schedule is in Section 6 of the Privacy Policy.

---

## **8. Your Rights**

At any time, you can:

* **review** the information we hold about your child;
* **correct** it;
* **delete** it, together with the account; and
* **refuse further collection**, by disconnecting or by asking us to close the account.

Email **support@lyceon.ai** from the address on your parent or guardian account. We may need to verify that you are the connected parent or guardian before acting on your request.

---

## **9. Changes**

If we make a material change to how we collect, use or disclose your child's information, we will notify you and, where the law requires, obtain your consent before the change takes effect.
