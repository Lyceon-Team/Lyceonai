# **LYCEON Children's Online Privacy Notice**

**For parents and guardians of students under 13**

This notice explains, in plain language, what LYCEON collects from a student under 13, how we use it, and what you can do. It is part of the **LYCEON Privacy Policy**, which covers everything not mentioned here.

---

## **1. Who We Are**

LYCEON AI operates the LYCEON SAT-preparation service.

* Email: **support@lyceon.ai**
* Postal address: **[POSTAL ADDRESS — TO BE PROVIDED]**
* Telephone: **[TELEPHONE — TO BE PROVIDED]**

---

## **2. Students Under 13 Need You**

LYCEON is designed for students aged 13 and over. A student under 13 can use LYCEON's learning features **only while a parent or guardian is connected to their account.**

* **Before you connect,** your child's account can only be used to connect you and to manage the account. We hold only what was needed to create it: name, email address, date of birth and sign-in details, plus the record of the sign-up agreements and basic technical data such as IP address. No learning activity is collected.
* **If your child asks us to,** we email you their link code at the address they type in. We use your address only to send that email and to limit how often it can be sent.
* **You connect** by entering your child's link code in your own LYCEON account and agreeing to the **LYCEON Parent / Guardian Terms**. You must be at least 18.
* **If you disconnect,** and no other parent or guardian is connected, the learning features close again.

---

## **3. What We Collect While You Are Connected**

* **Account information:** name, email address, date of birth, sign-in details.
* **Learning activity:** answers to practice and test questions, progress, and time spent.
* **Technical information:** device and browser type, IP address, and the cookies needed to keep your child signed in.

We collect only what is reasonably needed for your child to use LYCEON, and we do not require more information as a condition of taking part.

---

## **4. What We Do Not Do**

* **No AI tutor.** LISA, our AI tutor, is not available to anyone under 13, so no tutor conversations exist and nothing is sent to an AI service.
* **No analytics or session recording.** Under-13 accounts are excluded from product analytics and session recording.
* **No marketing.** We never send marketing messages to a student under 13.
* **No advertising.** We do not show advertising, do not use your child's information for advertising, and do not sell or share it.
* **No public profile.** Your child cannot publish personal information to other users.

---

## **5. How We Use It**

To run the service for your child: show their practice and progress, show you what you are entitled to see, keep the account secure, and fix problems.

---

## **6. Who Else Sees It**

* **You, and any other connected parent or guardian**, as described in the Privacy Policy, Section 5.1.
* **Service providers that run LYCEON for us**, only on our instructions and only for that purpose: database and sign-in (Supabase), hosting (Vercel), email delivery (Resend), and the math calculator (Desmos), which receives technical browser information when it loads. The full list is in the **LYCEON Sub-Processor List**.
* **Where the law requires it**, or to protect someone's safety.

---

## **7. How Long We Keep It**

* While the account is open, we keep your child's information so the service works.
* When the account is deleted, we follow the Privacy Policy, Section 6.2: identifying information is erased after a 7-day window in which you can change your mind, and the learning records that remain are de-identified so they no longer connect to your child.
* Our full retention schedule is in the Privacy Policy, Section 6.

---

## **8. Your Rights**

At any time, you can:

* **review** the information we hold about your child;
* **correct** it;
* **delete** it, and the account; and
* **stop further collection**, by disconnecting or by asking us to close the account.

Email **support@lyceon.ai** from the address on your parent or guardian account. We may need to confirm you are the connected parent or guardian before acting.

---

## **9. Changes**

If we make a material change to how we collect, use or share your child's information, we will tell you first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again.

## Counsel checklist

1. **Verifiable parental consent (SCL-187, SCL-051).** Is redeeming the student's link code, plus accepting the Parent / Guardian Terms, a consent method acceptable under 16 CFR §312.5(b)? This is the open question SCL-187 routes to counsel. Because the student's information is disclosed to processors, the "email plus" method does not apply.
2. **Pre-consent data.** Before a parent connects, the account holds the child's name, email address and date of birth. Confirm whether §312.5(c) permits holding these without consent, and for how long. If not, decide the deletion period (see Privacy Policy v5 checklist item 2).
3. **Operator contact details.** §312.4(d)(1) requires a postal address and telephone number. Placeholders are in Section 1; Karl will supply them. Do not publish until they are filled in.
4. **Retention policy.** The 2025 amendments require a written retention policy in the notice (§312.10). Confirm that referencing Privacy Policy Section 6 is sufficient, or whether it must be restated here.
5. **Under-13 learning data in de-identified form.** Confirm that keeping de-identified learning records after deletion (Section 7) is consistent with §312.10 for under-13 accounts.
6. **Jurisdictions.** Doc 10 §9.14 also names the Ireland 16+ digital consent age, the Australian Children's Online Privacy Code, and the UK Children's Code. Decide whether to add jurisdiction-specific sections here or in the Privacy Policy.
