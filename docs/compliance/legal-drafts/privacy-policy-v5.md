# **LYCEON Privacy Policy**

**Version 5** · **Effective date:** [●]

LYCEON respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect information when students, parents and guardians use the LYCEON platform.

This policy applies to students using LYCEON, parents or guardians connected to a student account, people who pay for a subscription, and visitors to the LYCEON website or app.

---

## **1. Information We Collect**

### **1.1 Information You Provide**

* Name or username
* Email address
* Date of birth
* Account credentials, or your Google account if you sign in with Google
* A parent's or guardian's email address, if a student asks us to send them a link code
* Messages, responses or feedback submitted through the platform, including private feedback you send to the LYCEON team
* Reviews you choose to leave about LYCEON: a star rating, optional text, and whether LYCEON may quote the review anonymously
* Your choice about marketing emails, and when and where you made it

### **1.2 Learning and Usage Data**

* Answers submitted to questions
* Progress and performance data
* Time spent on activities
* Interaction with explanations and AI tools

### **1.3 Technical and Device Data**

* Device type and browser
* IP address
* Log data and error reports
* Cookies and similar technologies, as described in the **LYCEON Cookie Policy**

### **1.4 Billing Information**

**We do not store payment card numbers.** Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe receives and stores the payer's name, email address, billing address and payment method details. We store subscription details, such as the subscription status, the billing period and the student account the subscription applies to.

---

## **2. How We Use Information**

We use information to:

* provide and operate the LYCEON platform;
* personalize learning, for example by tracking progress by skill;
* improve explanations and content quality;
* monitor platform performance, reliability and security;
* prevent abuse, cheating or misuse;
* process payments and manage subscriptions;
* invite customers to review LYCEON, and read the reviews and private feedback they send to improve the service;
* quote a review anonymously, only if its author allowed it;
* keep a record of marketing email choices, so we can honor them and show when consent was given or withdrawn; and
* send service messages, and marketing messages only if you opt in (see the **LYCEON Marketing Communications Consent**).

**We do not use personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell personal information.**

---

## **3. Artificial Intelligence**

LYCEON uses artificial intelligence to support learning. The **LYCEON AI Content Disclosure** explains what this means for you.

### **3.1 Personalization**

Learning data is used to adapt explanations and practice to the student.

### **3.2 Service Improvement**

We may analyze data in aggregated, de-identified or pseudonymized form to improve the quality, accuracy, safety and reliability of the service.

### **3.3 Third-Party AI Services**

Conversations with LISA, our AI tutor, are processed by Google Cloud. Data shared with these services is limited to what is needed for the conversation, and Google is contractually prohibited from using it to train public or general-purpose models.

**No information from a user under 13 is sent to any third-party AI service.**

---

## **4. Children's Privacy**

LYCEON is designed for students aged 13 and over. A student under 13 may use LYCEON only as described in this Section. The **LYCEON Children's Online Privacy Notice** gives parents further detail.

### **4.1 Parent or Guardian Connection**

A student under 13 cannot use any learning feature until a parent or guardian has connected to their account.

Until then, the account holds only the information needed to create it (name, email address, date of birth and sign-in details), the record of the sign-up agreements, and the technical data described in Section 1.3. No learning activity is collected.

If the student asks us to, we email their link code to the parent or guardian address they provide. We use that address only to send the email and to prevent misuse of this feature.

A parent or guardian connects by entering the student's link code in their own account and agreeing to the **LYCEON Parent / Guardian Terms**. A parent or guardian must be at least 18.

If the last connected parent or guardian disconnects, the student's learning features are no longer available.

### **4.2 AI Tutor**

LISA is not available to users under 13.

### **4.3 Information Collected From Users Under 13**

While a parent or guardian is connected, the information described in Section 1 is collected, except that an account of a user under 13:

* has no tutor conversations;
* is excluded from analytics and session recording;
* never receives marketing messages;
* never receives review invitations and cannot leave a review; and
* may send us private feedback, which we use only to improve the service.

### **4.4 Parent and Guardian Rights**

A connected parent or guardian may review, correct or delete their child's information, or ask us to stop collecting it, at any time by contacting **support@lyceon.ai**.

Disconnecting ends the child's access to learning features. You may ask us to delete the child's information at the same time.

### **4.5 Consent**

We obtain a parent's or guardian's consent to an under-13 student's use of LYCEON when the parent or guardian connects to the student's account.

## **5. How We Share Information**

We share information only where necessary to operate the platform, where you direct us to, or where the law requires it.

### **5.1 With a Connected Parent or Guardian**

A connected parent or guardian can see the student's progress and practice activity, skill-level progress, practice test results and study plan. Some of this is available only while the student has a paid subscription.

A connected parent or guardian cannot see the student's conversations with LISA.

### **5.2 Service Providers**

We share information with the following service providers, which process it only on our instructions. The **LYCEON Sub-Processor List** gives further detail.

| Provider | Purpose | Information processed |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Database and authentication | Account data, learning data |
| **Vercel** | Application hosting and delivery | Request and log data, including IP address |
| **Stripe** | Payment processing | Payer name, email address, billing address, payment method |
| **Google Cloud** | AI services and content safety; background processing | Tutor conversation content (users 13 and over); internal identifiers |
| **Google** | Sign in with Google, if you choose it | Google sign-in details |
| **Resend** | Email delivery | Email address and message content |
| **Desmos** | Graphing calculator | Technical browser data, such as IP address and browser type |
| **Slack** | Internal team notifications | Internal reference numbers |
| **PostHog** | Product analytics and session recording | Usage events and page interactions, as described in Section 9 |
| **Cloudflare** | Security and bot protection (Turnstile) | Technical browser data |
| **Trustpilot** | Customer reviews | Name, email address and a reference number, to invite you to leave a review |

Providers of AI services are contractually prohibited from using personal information to train public or general-purpose models.

### **5.3 Legal and Safety Reasons**

We may disclose information where required to comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety or security of LYCEON, our users or others.

Where a tutor conversation raises a safety concern, we may review it and contact a connected parent or guardian or, where necessary, the appropriate authorities, as described in **Trust & Safety at LYCEON**.

### **5.4 No Sale or Sharing**

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. See the **LYCEON Notice of Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing**.

### **5.5 Schools**

Where a school or district contracts with LYCEON, we process student information on the school's behalf under a written data privacy agreement. In that role we act as a "school official" under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA): we use student records only to provide the service to the school, we do not sell them or use them for advertising, and we return or delete them when the agreement ends. Parents of students using LYCEON through a school should contact the school to exercise their FERPA rights. See the **LYCEON School Data Privacy Addendum**.

## **6. Data Retention**

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, and we delete or de-identify it as set out below.

### **6.1 Account and Learning Data**

While your account is open, we keep your profile, practice and test activity, and progress data.

Tutor conversations are kept while your subscription is active and are deleted seven days after it ends, unless you resubscribe within those seven days. If you have never subscribed, they are kept while your account is open and deleted when your account is deleted.

In-app notifications are kept for 90 days.

### **6.2 Account Deletion**

You may cancel a deletion request within 7 days of making it.

After 7 days, we permanently delete the information that identifies you, including your name, email address, date of birth and sign-in details. Your learning activity is de-identified so that it can no longer be linked to you.

We retain:

* **a record of your deletion request**, including your email address, for 24 months, after which the email address is removed;
* **records of consents given on the account**, for as long as necessary to demonstrate that consent was obtained; the IP address and browser information attached to them are removed 24 months after your deletion request is completed; and
* **payment records**, for 7 years, to meet tax and financial record-keeping obligations.

If you ask us not to contact you again, we keep your email address on a suppression list so that we can honour that request.

### **6.3 Billing Terms Consent**

Records of consent to the Billing Terms are kept for at least three years from the date of consent, or one year after the subscription ends, whichever is longer.

### **6.4 Children**

Information from an account of a user under 13 is kept for the periods in this Section. A parent or guardian may ask us to delete it at any time (Section 4.4).

### **6.5 Security Records**

Records of sign-ins, security events and administrative actions are kept for 365 days. When you delete your account, your identity is removed from these records.

### **6.6 Analytics**

Analytics events are kept for [●] months and session recordings for [●] days. We may keep aggregated analytics data that no longer relates to any individual.

### **6.7 Other Operational Records**

Other operational records that include personal information are kept for no more than 90 days.

## **7. Data Security and Breach Notification**

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information, including secure authentication, access controls, and monitoring and logging.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach compromises personal information, we will notify affected users and, where applicable, connected parents or guardians, in accordance with applicable law.

## **8. Your Rights and Choices**

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, request a portable copy, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent.

Parents and guardians may exercise these rights on behalf of a connected student.

### **8.1 California Residents**

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to delete it; to correct it; to obtain a portable copy; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. The **LYCEON California Notice at Collection** lists the categories we collect.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We honor the Global Privacy Control signal (Section 9).

### **8.2 European Union, EEA and United Kingdom Residents**

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal information on the following legal bases:

* **performance of a contract**, to operate your account and subscription;
* **legitimate interests**, for platform security, abuse prevention, service improvement and review invitations;
* **consent**, for a parent's or guardian's connection to a student account, for non-essential cookies, analytics and session recording, for marketing messages, and for quoting a review; and
* **legal obligation**, where required.

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, the right to withdraw consent at any time, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

### **8.3 Making a Request**

Contact **support@lyceon.ai**. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and may need to verify your identity first.

## **9. Cookies, Analytics and Session Recording**

We use cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in, protect your account and remember your settings. These do not require your consent.

**We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.**

We use PostHog for product analytics and session recording:

* **Your choice.** We ask whether you accept analytics cookies when you first visit. You can change your choice at any time using the **Cookie settings** link.
* **Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal.
* **Session recording.** Session recording runs only if you accept analytics cookies. Text you enter is masked, and recordings do not include conversations with LISA.
* **Children.** Accounts of users under 13 are excluded from analytics and session recording.
* **Limited data.** We do not send names or email addresses to PostHog, and IP addresses are not stored.

The **LYCEON Cookie Policy** lists the cookies we use and how long they last.
