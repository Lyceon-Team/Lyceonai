# **LYCEON Parental Consent**

**Version 1** · **Effective date:** 2026-10-07

## **1. Connection Process**

1. The student creates an account and provides their date of birth. If the student is under 13, learning features are unavailable until a parent or guardian connects.
2. The student gives their parent or guardian a link code. At the student's request, LYCEON emails the code to an address the student provides.
3. The parent or guardian signs in to their own LYCEON account. Parents and guardians must be at least 18.
4. The parent or guardian enters the link code, accepts the consent statement in Section 2, and submits. The student's learning features then become available.
5. If the last connected parent or guardian disconnects, the student's learning features are no longer available.

## **2. Consent Statement**

Shown unticked. The request cannot be submitted until it is ticked.

> ☐ I am this student's parent or legal guardian, and I am at least 18. I have read and agree to the **Parent / Guardian Terms**, and I have read the **Privacy Policy** and the **Children's Online Privacy Notice**. I consent to my child's use of LYCEON as described in those documents, including the collection and use of my child's learning activity to provide the service.

Shown below the statement:

> You can disconnect, or ask us to delete your child's information, at any time. Email **support@lyceon.ai**.

For a student aged 13 to 17, the last sentence of the statement reads: "I agree to my child's use of LYCEON as described in those documents."

## **3. Consent Record**

For each consent, LYCEON records:

* the document and version accepted;
* the date and time;
* the parent or guardian account that gave consent; and
* the IP address and browser used.

Consent records are retained as described in Section 6.2 of the Privacy Policy.

## **4. Identity Verification**

Consent relies on the parent's or guardian's acceptance, the age requirement for parent and guardian accounts, and the student's link code.

## Decisions (Karl, 2026-10-07)

1. Consent method: the connection process in Sections 1 to 4 (an 18+ parent or guardian account, the student's link code, and the unticked consent statement) is LYCEON's consent method at launch, as ruled (R6; SCL-187, approved 2026-10-05). No additional method is added.
2. Payment-card verification: not used at launch.
3. Consent statement, including the 13 to 17 variant: approved as written.
4. Consent record: adequate; kept permanently (SCL-215; Privacy Policy Section 6.2).
5. Jurisdictions with a digital age of consent above 13: not applicable; the service is US-only at launch.
