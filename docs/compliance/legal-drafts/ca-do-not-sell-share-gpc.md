# **Your Privacy Choices: Sale and Sharing**

**Version 1** · **Effective date:** 2026-10-07

## **We Do Not Sell or Share Your Personal Information**

LYCEON **does not sell** personal information and **does not share** it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. We have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not show advertising.

This includes the personal information of consumers under 16.

## **Service Providers**

We use service providers for hosting, database, payments, email, AI services, product analytics, security and customer reviews. They process personal information only on our instructions and only to provide services to us, under contracts that prohibit them from using it for their own purposes. They are listed in the **LYCEON Sub-Processor List**.

## **Global Privacy Control**

**We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.** If your browser sends GPC, we treat it as:

* a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information; and
* a refusal of analytics cookies, so analytics and session recording do not run in that browser.

## **Changes to Our Practices**

If we intend to sell or share personal information, we will update this notice and provide a way to opt out before doing so. For consumers under 16, we will obtain opt-in consent as required by law.
