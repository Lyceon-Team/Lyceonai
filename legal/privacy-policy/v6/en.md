# **LYCEON Privacy Policy**

LYCEON respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when students, parents, and guardians use the LYCEON platform.

This policy applies to students using LYCEON, parents or guardians connected to a student account, people who pay for a subscription, and visitors to the LYCEON website or app.

By using LYCEON, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## **1. Information We Collect**

### **1.1 Information You Provide**

* Name or username
* Email address
* Account credentials
* A guardian email address, where a student provides one at sign-up
* Messages, responses, or feedback submitted through the platform, including private feedback you send to the LYCEON team
* Reviews you choose to leave about LYCEON: a star rating, optional text, and whether LYCEON may quote the review anonymously
* Your choice about marketing emails, and when and where you made it

### **1.2 Learning and Usage Data**

* Answers submitted to questions
* Progress and performance data
* Time spent on activities
* Interaction patterns with explanations and AI tools

### **1.3 Technical and Device Data**

* Device type and browser
* IP address
* Log data and error reports
* Cookies and similar technologies, as described in Section 9
* If you accept analytics cookies, how you first arrived at LYCEON (for example, from a search engine or an ad), as described in Section 9

### **1.4 Billing Information**

**We do not store payment card numbers.** Payments are processed by Stripe, and card details go directly to Stripe without passing through LYCEON's systems.

When a subscription is purchased, **Stripe receives and stores** the payer's name, email address, billing address, and payment method details. **LYCEON stores** the payer's Stripe customer identifier, the subscription identifier, the subscription status and billing period, and which student account the subscription funds.

We also store a record of the payer's consent to the Billing Terms — the date and time, the version agreed to, and the account it belongs to — as described in Section 6.3.

## **2. How We Use Information**

We use collected data to:

* Provide and operate the LYCEON platform
* Personalize learning experiences
* Improve explanations, content quality, and difficulty targeting
* Monitor platform performance and security
* Prevent abuse, cheating, or misuse
* Process payments and manage subscriptions
* Communicate important updates or notices
* Understand how the website and app are used, and test improvements to them, if you accept analytics cookies (Section 9)
* Read the reviews and private feedback you send us, to improve the service
* Quote a review anonymously, only if its author allowed it
* Keep a record of marketing email choices, so we can honor them and show when consent was given or withdrawn
* Send marketing emails, only if you opt in

**We do not use personal data for targeted advertising, and we do not sell personal data.**

## **3. AI and Data Usage**

LYCEON uses artificial intelligence to support learning and personalization.

### **3.1 AI Personalization**

Learning data may be used to adapt explanations and hints, adjust difficulty levels, and improve clarity and instructional effectiveness.

### **3.2 Model Improvement**

LYCEON may analyze data in aggregated, anonymized, or pseudonymized form to improve platform quality and enhance AI accuracy, safety, and reliability.

### **3.3 Third-Party AI Providers**

Conversations with LISA, our AI tutor, are processed by Google Cloud Vertex AI. Data shared with that service is limited to what is needed to generate a response, and Google is contractually prohibited from using it to train public or general-purpose models.

**No data from a user under 13 is sent to any third-party AI provider**, because LISA is not available below that age. See Section 4.

## **4. Children's Privacy**

LYCEON is designed for students aged 13 and over. Users under 13 may use LYCEON only under the conditions in this Section.

### **4.1 Under-13 Accounts Require a Connected Guardian**

A student under 13 **cannot use LYCEON until a parent or guardian has connected their account.**

Before that connection exists, the only information we hold about the student is the guardian email address they provided at sign-up, which we use solely to invite their guardian. No learning data, no usage data, and no platform activity is collected, because the account is not active.

When a guardian connects and agrees to the Parent / Guardian Terms, the account activates.

### **4.2 No AI Tutor Below 13**

**LISA is not available to any user under 13.** This means no conversation data from an under-13 account is created, stored, or transmitted to Google Cloud Vertex AI or any other third-party AI provider.

### **4.3 What We Collect From an Under-13 Account**

Once active, an under-13 account is subject to the same collection described in Section 1, **except** that an under-13 account:

* has no tutor conversations;
* is excluded from analytics and session recording;
* never receives marketing emails;
* is never asked to review LYCEON and cannot leave a review; and
* may send us private feedback, which we use only to improve the service.

### **4.4 Guardian Rights**

A connected guardian may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their student's data at any time by contacting **support@lyceon.ai**.

**Disconnecting ends the student's access** and you may request deletion of their data at the same time.

### **4.5 How We Treat This Consent**

Connecting a guardian account is how we obtain and record consent for an under-13 student's use of LYCEON. We describe this mechanism plainly rather than claiming it satisfies any particular legal standard for verifiable parental consent. If you have questions about how we handle your student's data, contact **support@lyceon.ai** and we will answer directly.

### **4.6 If We Learn an Account Is Under 13 Without a Guardian**

The account will be suspended, and associated personal data will be deleted in accordance with applicable law.

## **5. How We Share Information**

LYCEON shares information only where necessary to operate the platform, where you direct us to, or where the law requires it.

### **5.1 With a Connected Guardian**

**A linked parent or guardian can see the student's mastery level for each subject domain (for example, Algebra or Craft and Structure), the student's study calendar and progress, projected and target scores, and full-length practice test results. Guardians cannot see skill-level detail, individual answers, or conversations with LISA, our AI tutor.**

This sharing is a direct consequence of connecting, and either party can end the connection at any time.

| Provider | Purpose | What it receives |

### **5.3 Legal and Safety Reasons**

We may disclose information where required to comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, or security of LYCEON, our users, or others.

Where a tutor conversation raises a safety concern, we may review it and contact a connected guardian or, where necessary, appropriate authorities. This is described further in **Trust & Safety at LYCEON**.

### **5.4 We Do Not Sell Personal Information**

LYCEON does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## **6. How Long We Keep Your Information**

We keep information only as long as we need it, and we delete or anonymize it on the schedule below. We do not keep any category of information indefinitely except where this section says so explicitly.

### **6.1 Your Account and Learning Data**

While your account is open, we keep your profile, your practice and test activity, and your progress data so the service works.

Tutor conversations are kept while your subscription is active. If your subscription ends, they are deleted seven days later. If you resubscribe within those seven days, they are kept. If you have never subscribed, they are kept while your account is open and are deleted when your account is deleted.

Conversations flagged for safety review are kept until our safety review team has completed its review and closed the case. They are then deleted by that team. While a review is open, these records may be kept longer than our standard retention periods.

Notifications you receive in the app are kept for 90 days.

Reviews, private feedback and the record of your marketing email choices are kept while your account is open and are deleted when your account is deleted.

If your account is inactive for 12 months, we delete it. Before deleting an inactive account, we will email you at least 48 hours in advance (and, for a student, their linked parent or guardian). Signing in or using Lyceon during that time cancels the deletion.

### **6.2 When You Delete Your Account**

You have 7 days to change your mind. Nothing is erased during that window and you can cancel at any time.

After 7 days we permanently erase the information that identifies you — your name, email address, date of birth, and login. Your learning activity is anonymized: the records remain, but nothing in them connects to you, and we cannot reverse that.

The one exception is conversations flagged for safety review. These are disconnected from the deleted account and kept only until the safety review is closed, as described in Section 6.1.

Three things survive, and here is exactly why:

* A record that you asked us to delete your account, including your email address, kept for 24 months. We are required to keep records of these requests. After 24 months we remove the email address and keep only the date and outcome.
* A record that a parent or guardian gave consent, kept permanently. This is how we can show consent was properly obtained.
* Payment records, kept for 7 years, because tax and financial rules require it.

If you asked us not to contact you again, we keep your email address on a do-not-contact list until you tell us otherwise. That is the only way we can honour the request.

### **6.3 Consent to the Billing Terms**

Records of consent to the Billing Terms are kept for no less than three years from the date of consent, or one year after the subscription ends, whichever is longer. This is a separate record from the parent or guardian consent described above, and it is kept on its own clock.

### **6.4 Children**

A child under 13 may use LYCEON only with an active guardian connection, as described in Section 4. If we identify an account belonging to a child under 13 without one, we delete it.

### **6.5 Security and Operational Records**

Records of sign-ins, security events and administrative actions are kept for 365 days. When you delete your account, your identity is removed from these records immediately; the records themselves remain for the rest of that period. Records of a parent or guardian connecting to or disconnecting from a student are the exception: they are consent records and are kept permanently (Section 6.2).

Records of how our systems were configured are kept permanently, as an operational record. These describe our operations, not you.

### **6.6 Analytics**

We use PostHog to understand how the service is used, only if you accept analytics cookies, and never for users under 13. Analytics events are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Session recordings are kept for 30 days.

### **6.7 Anything Else**

Where we keep operational records that include information about you and are not listed above, we keep them for no more than 90 days.

### **6.8 Changes**

If we change these periods we will update this section and note the date.

## **9. Cookies, Analytics and Session Recording**

LYCEON uses cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in, protect your account, and remember your settings. These do not require your consent.

**We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.**

We use PostHog for product analytics and session recording, on our website and on pages within the signed-in app:

* **Your choice.** We ask whether you accept analytics cookies when you first visit. You can change your choice at any time using the **Cookie settings** link. We keep a record of each choice — whether analytics was accepted, the version of the notice shown, and when — which does not include your name, your account or your IP address.
* **Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal.
* **Limited data.** We do not send names or email addresses to PostHog, IP addresses are anonymized, and analytics never includes your answers to questions or your conversations with LISA.
* **Session recording.** Session recording runs only if you accept analytics cookies. Recordings mask everything you type, question and answer areas (in practice, review, practice tests and the Question of the Day), conversations with LISA, and the text of reviews and feedback.
* **How you found us.** If you accept analytics cookies, we note the kind of source you first arrived from — direct, a referring website, a paid ad, a search engine, or unknown — for the rest of that browser session, and store it with your account if you sign up. We do not keep the address of the page you came from or the name of any campaign.
* **Testing improvements.** If you accept analytics cookies, we may show you one of several versions of a page, such as the homepage, to learn which works better.
* **Children.** Accounts of users under 13 are excluded from analytics and session recording.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly if disabled.

### **9.1 Question of the Day**

You can answer the Question of the Day without an account. To protect it from automated abuse, we use Cloudflare Turnstile, and we limit how often it can be used from one connection using a keyed one-way code derived from your IP address, which we delete within two days; we never store the IP address itself. We keep only daily totals of how many answers were submitted and how many were correct. We do not keep anyone's individual answers.

### **9.2 Marketing Emails**

Users aged 13 and over may choose to receive marketing emails. We send them only if you opt in, and we record when you opted in or out, where you did so, and the version of the wording you agreed to. You can opt out at any time in your account settings, and every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Users under 13 never receive marketing emails.
