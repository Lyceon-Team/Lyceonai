## **3. Age Requirements and Under-13 Accounts**

### **3.1 Ages 13 to 18**

**LYCEON is a student-first platform.** A student aged 13 or older may create an account and use every feature of LYCEON independently. They agree to the Student Terms of Use directly, and your consent is not a precondition for their use.

These Parent Terms apply to you from the moment you **connect your account to theirs** or **pay for their subscription** — not before.

**Your student controls the connection.** They generate the link code and choose whether to share it with you, and either of you can end the connection at any time. You cannot connect to a student's account without their participation. We have built it this way deliberately: a student decides who sees their work.

Anyone who pays for a subscription must be at least 18. See Section 9.

### **3.2 Under 13 — Your Connection Is Required**

**A student under 13 cannot use LYCEON until you have connected your account to theirs.**

Before you connect:

* Their account is not active
* They cannot use any platform feature
* LYCEON collects nothing from them beyond the guardian email address they provide at sign-up, which is used solely to invite you

When you connect your account and agree to these Parent Terms:

* Their account activates
* **They will not have access to LISA, our AI tutor.** This applies to every LYCEON user under 13, without exception. No data from an under-13 account is sent to any third-party AI provider
* All other features become available to them

**If you disconnect your account, their access ends.**

### **3.3 What This Consent Is**

Connecting your account is how you consent to your student's use of LYCEON, and it is how we record that consent. We describe this plainly rather than claiming it satisfies any particular legal standard for verifiable parental consent. If you have questions about how we handle your student's data, the **Privacy Policy** describes it in full and **support@lyceon.ai** will answer directly.

## **4. What You Can and Cannot See**

### **4.1 What a Connected Guardian Can See**

* Progress and practice activity
* Mastery breakdown by subject and skill
* Score projections and exam results
* Study calendar

### **4.2 What a Connected Guardian Cannot See**

**You cannot see your student's conversations with LISA.** Tutor conversations are private to the student.

This is deliberate. A student who believes a parent is reading every question they ask will stop asking the questions they most need to ask. We think a student learns more when that space is private, and we have built the platform that way.

**This does not mean those conversations are unmonitored.** LYCEON applies automated safety systems and human review to tutor conversations, and we will contact you directly where a safety concern requires it, as described in the **Trust & Safety** page and the Privacy Policy.

## **5. How Connecting Works**

Your student generates a **link code** in their account settings and shares it with you. You enter that code in your guardian account and the connection is active immediately.

Link codes expire after 24 hours and stop working once used. Your student can generate a new one at any time.

**Either you or your student can end the connection at any time**, from account settings. When a connection ends, you immediately lose access to their data. If your student is under 13, ending the connection also ends their access to LYCEON.

You may connect to more than one student. Each student generates their own code and each connection is independent.
