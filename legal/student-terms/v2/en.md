## **4. Connecting a Parent or Guardian**

### **4.1 Your Link Code**

Your account settings display a **link code** — a short code you can share with a parent or guardian so they can connect their account to yours.

**Sharing your link code gives whoever holds it guardian access to your account.** Share it only with a parent or guardian you intend to have that access.

Your link code expires 24 hours after it is issued and stops working as soon as someone uses it. You can generate a new one at any time from your account settings.

### **4.2 What a Connected Guardian Can See**

A connected guardian can see:

* Your progress and practice activity
* Your mastery breakdown by subject and skill
* Your score projections and exam results
* Your study calendar

### **4.3 What a Connected Guardian Cannot See**

A connected guardian **cannot see your conversations with LISA**. Tutor conversations are private to you.

This is a deliberate design choice. LYCEON exists to help you learn, and that includes being able to ask questions you would not ask in front of someone else.

### **4.4 Ending a Connection**

**Either you or your guardian can end the connection at any time**, from account settings. When a connection ends, the guardian immediately loses access to your data.

If you are under 13, ending the connection also ends your access to LYCEON, because a connected guardian is required at that age.
