# Lyceon hook bank (living brainstorm, v1, 2026-10-15)

Long-term angles for content, ads, video and Reddit. Each is a real bottleneck, the Lyceon feature that solves it (live today), and draft hooks. This is a brainstorm bank: hooks are starting points, not approved copy.

## Rules

- Problem → solution framing, always tied to a feature that is live today.
- No promised score outcomes.
- Any figure (prices, fees, tutor rates) needs a cited source before use.
- New angles are added in the same columns, marked `proposed` until Karl approves.

## The psychology (why these work)

- **Reframe the unit:** price in tutor-hours or cost per day, a unit buyers already understand (the horsepower principle).
- **Remove uncertainty, not effort:** "tonight's plan" beats "lots of practice".
- **Borrow a familiar ritual:** dress rehearsal or fire drill for test day.
- **Make progress visible to someone else:** peace of mind for parents, proof for students.
- **Start absurdly small:** one question a day lowers the barrier to zero.

## How an angle expands

- **Ad:** hook, one-line solution, call to action; one ad set per angle.
- **Short video:** the bottleneck in 3 seconds, the Lyceon screen that solves it, the hook.
- **Blog:** the bottleneck as the reader's question, a practical answer, a soft pointer to the feature.
- **Landing page:** one angle per page or hero variant, tested in PostHog.
- **Reddit:** the bottleneck as an honest discussion in Karl's voice; the product only when relevant.
