# Brag Plan: Lyceon

## What is this app?
Lyceon turns Digital SAT preparation into one connected loop: establish a baseline, practise adaptively, understand mistakes, build measured mastery, and keep students and guardians aligned.

## The angle
**One loop. Every next step earned.** Instead of presenting Lyceon as a bag of disconnected study tools, the film follows a single learner signal as it becomes a baseline, a targeted question, an explanation, a mastery update, and a clear next move. The marketable promise is continuity: practice never disappears into a score-shaped black box.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
A question card snaps into focus beside the line **“SAT prep shouldn’t feel random.”** The four-stage Lyceon loop draws itself beneath it.

## Key moments
- A diagnostic baseline turns into a focused Math practice session.
- One submitted answer unlocks an explanation and a contextual tutor follow-up.
- The observed attempt updates domain mastery and the dashboard recommends the next block.
- A linked guardian receives the same progress truth as a read-only view.

## Outro / punchline
**Lyceon. Know what to practise next — and why.**

## User flow worth showing
Take a baseline diagnostic → practise a targeted question → submit and review a step-by-step explanation → ask the tutor in context → see mastery and the next recommended block → optionally share read-only progress with a linked guardian.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium product film built around one continuous learning signal
- Interpretation: confident navy-and-cream frames, crisp UI choreography, restrained copy, and transitions that visually carry data from one product surface to the next.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The problem becomes a loop — 3.5s
A real-looking SAT question card appears beside “SAT prep shouldn’t feel random.” The path Baseline → Practice → Understand → Improve draws in.
Sequential/interaction: four stages arrive in order and hold.
Audio intent: intrigue, then confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: a single soft lock when the loop completes.
Transition mood: clean carry → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Baseline to target — 4.0s
A diagnostic result card resolves to Math 560 / Reading & Writing 610, then highlights “Linear equations” and opens a focused session.
Sequential/interaction: diagnostic completes, target chip travels into the practice header.
Audio intent: forward motion.
Audio-coupled idea: restrained confirmation cue.
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Practise and understand — 5.0s
The learner selects `x = 7`, submits, receives “Correct,” and a compact step-by-step explanation expands. “Ask Lyceon” opens with the question already in context.
Sequential/interaction: option select → submit → explanation → tutor prompt.
Audio intent: clarity and reward.
Audio-coupled idea: click plus soft success tone.
Transition mood: panel morph → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Every answer becomes direction — 4.0s
The attempt becomes an observed event; mastery for Algebra advances from Developing toward Proficient. The dashboard recommends “Complete one focused SAT practice block today.”
Sequential/interaction: event dot moves into mastery bar, then next-step card arrives.
Audio intent: measured momentum, never gamey.
Audio-coupled idea: low mastery step cue.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Progress everyone can trust — 3.5s
Student and linked guardian dashboards share the same weekly questions, accuracy, and domain mastery. A visible `VIEW ONLY` badge reinforces boundaries.
Sequential/interaction: student card duplicates into a guardian view only after link and entitlement locks illuminate.
Audio intent: trust and reassurance.
Audio-coupled idea: two quiet latch sounds.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 6.

### Scene 6 — Brand lockup — 4.0s
LYCEON appears above “Know what to practise next — and why.” The full loop rests beneath it with CTA “Start free practice.”
Sequential/interaction: loop resolves, CTA settles, long readable hold.
Audio intent: calm, resolved finish.
Audio-coupled idea: one warm logo hit.
Transition mood: fade to navy.

**Music mood for this video:** polished and quietly optimistic.
